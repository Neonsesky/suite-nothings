# w1-add-stay handoff

- [to: integrator] Shared changes, all additive: `LocationProvider` in `src/app/router.tsx`, and `Shell.tsx` wraps the background screen of a sheet route in it. This fixes "can't find that stay" behind `#/add` and is covered by e2e (`add-stay.spec.ts`: opening add over a stay detail keeps it). (files: src/app/router.tsx, src/app/Shell.tsx)
- [to: integrator] `BottomSheet`: new `onDismissAttempt` prop. Also a bug fix: a press on a footer or header button no longer starts a drag. Pointer capture on the panel used to swallow the click, so footer buttons never fired on touch or WebKit. (files: src/components/BottomSheet.tsx)
- [to: w1-backend] The `uploadPhoto` payload stays as in contracts: `{ photo, thumb_key, full_key }`. `photo` carries `photo_id, visit_id, width, height, taken_at, caption`. Load the blobs with `getPhotoBlob(photo_id, 'thumb'|'full')`, or read the `photoBlobs` rows through the new `blobOf(row)`, because WebKit may store `bytes` instead of `blob`. Keeping the blobs out of the outbox avoids storing every photo twice. After upload, write `thumb_file_id`/`full_file_id` back through `applied.photos`. (files: src/data/store.ts, src/data/types.ts)
- [to: w1-backend] `store.addPhoto` got `opts.processed` (it skips the pipeline when the sheet has already resized the photo). Saving a stay makes one `upsertHotel` (when new), one `upsertVisit`, and then per photo one `uploadPhoto` plus one `upsertVisit` (photo order). Offline, they all wait in the outbox. (files: src/data/store.ts)
- [to: w1-stays] Link "Edit" on stay detail to `#/add?edit=<visitId>`, and "Add another visit" to `#/add?hotel=<hotel_id>` (one tap: the hotel is preselected and the sheet opens on When). After saving, the sheet goes to `#/stay/<id>` (replace) unless it was opened from home. (files: src/features/add-stay/AddStaySheet.tsx)
- [to: w1-stays, integrator] `types.ts` `MOODS` now also includes the design deck's `giggly` and `fizzy`; the picker uses `components/brand/MoodStamps` ids. `giddy`/`sleepy` remain valid for old rows. Render moods with `MoodStamp`, which falls back to Blissful for unknown ids. (files: src/data/types.ts)
- [to: w1-map] The add sheet uses `MiniMap` in two places: `interactive` + `onMove` for the manual pin (in a `data-no-drag` wrapper, 12 rem tall), and `dropPin` in the celebration (18×8.5 rem on ink). Until `onMove` fires, the pin stays at the start point: the "We're here now" GPS fix, the first search result, or home base. (files: src/features/add-stay/HotelStep.tsx, Celebration.tsx)
- [to: later wave: milestones] Added `src/features/milestones/index.ts` with `showMilestoneUnlock()` as a no-op. The sheet calls `checkMilestones(before, [])` for `prev`, then `checkMilestones(after, prev)`, and hands anything new to `showMilestoneUnlock` after the celebration. (files: src/features/milestones/index.ts)
- [to: integrator] `vite.config.ts`: removed the `exifr` manual chunk. It captured Vite's preload helper, so the entry modulepreloaded all of exifr (+26 KB). exifr is still lazy, in its own `full.esm-*.js` chunk. (files: vite.config.ts)
- [to: later wave: enrichment] New hotels are created with `enrichment_status: 'pending'` and `requestEnrichment(hotelId)` runs in the background. OSM `brand`, `wikidata`, `stars`, `website` and `phone` come from Photon `extra` tags. (files: src/features/add-stay/save.ts, draft.ts)
- Known gaps: captions of photos already saved are read-only in edit mode (no photo-update API), and removing a saved photo in edit mode only drops it from `photo_ids_json` (the Photo row stays). The desktop sheet is the centred `BottomSheet` modal, not the 560 px right panel from the wireframe.
