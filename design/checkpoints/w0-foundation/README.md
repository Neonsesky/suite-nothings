- `stays-*.png`: Stays v1 at 390, 412 and 1440 (`stays-full-desktop-1440.png` is the full page).
- `welcome-iphone-390.png`: first launch, "Who's checking in?".
- `us-iphone-390.png`: the Us stub.
- `add-sheet-*.png`: `#/add` as a bottom sheet over Stays.
- `sheet-half` / `sheet-full` (iPhone) and `sheet-modal` (desktop): BottomSheet snap points and the desktop modal.
- `gallery-desktop-1440.png`: every core component (`#/gallery`).
- `splitflap-frames-desktop-1440/`: frames mid-flip (the fold, the riffle glyph and the landing).
- `splitflap.webm`: a recording of the counter and chapter boards flipping.
